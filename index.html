      How to play

      You are the unseen god of a small walled sanctuary. Its people live their own lives: they eat, sleep, work, train, fall in love, raise children, discover new things and build their home up, building by building. Every one of them has a story.

      Watch. Drag to look around, pinch to zoom, double-tap to zoom in. Tap anyone for their character sheet: thoughts, skills, relationships and life story. Tribe shows the whole sanctuary, History everything that has happened (share its chronicle as an image from there or the Throne).

      Act. Bless heals someone and doubles how fast they learn. Inspire sends a dream that leads to discoveries. Omen sets what the whole tribe focuses on. People who see miracles grow devout, and the devout pray, which gives you Faith.

      Wrath. Smite, Storm and Pestilence are the darker side of godhood. Striking the wicked looks like justice; striking the innocent teaches your people to fear you. The fearful work harder, steal less and pray to appease you, but they are unhappier. The Tribe sheet shows whether your people love or fear you.

      The throne. Every family is a house. When the ruler dies the crown passes by blood, and once it has passed from parent to child the house is royal. Rulers may take several spouses. Make the ruler your puppet from the Throne, and decree marriages, the heir, buildings and callings. From anyone's sheet you can also Gift a talent or lost knowledge, or grant eternal youth.

      Society. What people make of themselves earns them standing and a rank, from Commoner up to Noble. Rank brings finer meals, a seat at the Academy, market days and first claim on a new home, so the ambitious push harder, most of all when a friend rises past them. The hungry, the miserable and the envious may turn to theft or violence. The ruler judges them by the law of the land (set it by decree): shame, the stocks, the cells, or exile.

      Expansion. Only you can raise the walls. When the sanctuary has enough people, buildings and milestones (see the Tribe sheet), spend Faith to grow it into a Town and then a City: more land, homes and fields, and new buildings like the Academy, Watch House, Market and Cathedral.

      The Portal. Send up to five heroes into the dungeon for meat, herbs, ore and mana crystals. Match the floor's recommended power: death there is real. What they bring back lets your people forge iron and mithril, brew remedies and learn magic. Eight floors lead down to the Fallen King. Beware: monsters sometimes break out.

      Goals. The Tribe sheet's Goals tab shows the milestones of the current chapter. Each one reached rewards Faith.

      Character portraits: male and female sprites by Sutemo.

      Time. Pause or change the speed at the top right. The game saves itself every minute and whenever you leave.
