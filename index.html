      Game

      How to play

      You are the unseen god of a small walled sanctuary. Its people live their own lives: they eat, sleep, work, train, fall in love, raise children, discover new things and build their home up, building by building. Every one of them has a story.

      Watch

      Drag to look around, pinch to zoom. Tap anyone to see their character sheet, thoughts, relationships and life story. Tribe shows the whole sanctuary, Log everything that has happened.

      Act

      ✨ Bless heals someone and doubles how fast they learn. 💡 Inspire sends a dream that leads to discoveries. 🌠 Omen sets what the whole tribe focuses on. Miracles that people see grow their faith, and the faithful pray, which gives you more Faith.

      The Portal

      Tap 🌀 Portal (or the portal in the north wall) to send up to five heroes into the dungeon for meat, herbs, ore and mana crystals. Match the floor's recommended power. Death in the dungeon is real, but what they bring back lets your people forge iron, brew remedies and learn magic.

      Time

      Pause or speed up with the buttons at the top. The game autosaves every minute and when you leave; the ☰ menu has save slots and a new world.
